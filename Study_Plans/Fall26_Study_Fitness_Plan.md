## Problem-Triage Flowchart Reference

When starting any homework or exam problem, follow these 4 steps:

1. **Bound the System:** Draw boundary/control volume, define origin & coordinates ($+x, +y, +\theta$), identify state vs. process.
2. **Classify Domain & Laws:**
* *Dynamics:* $\sum F = ma$ vs. Work-Energy vs. Impulse-Momentum
* *Circuits:* DC vs. Transient vs. AC Phasor ($Z_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C}, Z_L = j\omega L$)
* *Optics:* Geometric ($\lambda \ll a$) vs. Physical/Wave Optics ($\lambda \approx a$)
* *Materials:* Elastic ($\sigma = E\epsilon$) vs. Plastic deformation vs. Phase Rule


3. **Variable Accounting:** Check if $N_{\text{equations}} = N_{\text{unknowns}}$. If $N_{\text{eq}} < N_{\text{unk}}$, find missing kinematic or geometric constraints before starting algebra.
4. **Symbolic Isolation & Unit Check:** Rearrange symbolically first. Verify left-hand units match right-hand units, then evaluate numerically.
